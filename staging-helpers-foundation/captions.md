## d1 Sat Nov 22 reel: Meet Kootenay Helpers

People want to help. They just can't find where.

Volunteer roles in the Kootenays are spread across hundreds of websites, one group and one town at a time. So I started building one place for all of them.

Kootenay Helpers is free. Pick your towns, tap the kind of help you'd like to give, and every role links straight back to the group that needs you. Every role comes from the groups' own pages, and every visit goes back to them.

The idea came from Nelson at its Best, and I couldn't stop thinking about it. I built it here in Castlegar. 400 roles from 99 groups so far. It grows every week.

Run a group that needs volunteers? Post a role free. Link in bio.

## d2 Sun Nov 23 reel: How to post a role

Here's what posting a volunteer role looks like, start to finish.

Tell me who you are and one way I can check your group. A website, a Facebook page or a phone number all work.
Then the role. What it's called, what a Helper does and when you need them.
Add where Helpers turn up and how they reach you.
Hit send.

It takes about five minutes, and you won't need a password. I read every role before it goes up. I reply within 24 hours. It goes live with the next morning's update.

Save this for the next time your group needs a hand.

## d3 Mon Nov 24 carousel: How to find a role

Looking for a way to help? Here's how to find one in about a minute.

Pick your town, or a few of them.
Tap the kind of help you'd like to give. Rinks, food banks, trails, boards, festivals and more.
Open a role to see when, where and who to contact.
Then reach the group straight from their own page.

You don't need an account. Tap Keep on any role to save it for later, or get one email on Sunday with new roles near you.

Send this to someone who keeps saying they'd like to help.

## d4 Tue Nov 25 carousel: Where these roles come from

Where do the roles on Kootenay Helpers come from? From the groups themselves.

Most of them I find on pages groups already share, like their own websites, their town hall's page, or volunteer sites like iVolunteer and Volunteer Now. Each role shows the facts the group's own page states and links straight back to them, so every visit goes to the people doing the work.

Run one of these groups? You can claim your listing and write it in your own words. If you'd rather not be listed, I'll take it down within two working days.

Thank you to every group out there keeping our towns running.

## d5 Wed Nov 26 carousel: Who can post

Who can post a role on Kootenay Helpers? More groups than you'd think.

Charities and non-profit societies. Service clubs, sports clubs and hall societies. Towns, the regional district, libraries, museums, fire halls and health programs. Community work run by a faith group. A small group with no website can post too, with a Facebook page or a phone number I can call.

Not listed: individuals asking for personal help, businesses, political campaigns, and roles only for parents of one school. Need a hand yourself? Call or text 2-1-1.

If your group is on the first list, there's no harm in trying.

## d6 Thu Nov 27 carousel: What your group gets

What does your group get from posting on Kootenay Helpers?

Every visit goes straight to your own page.
Every role gets its own share card, ready for your Facebook page or newsletter.
Once you have a role up, you get a patch for your website that links to all your open roles.
I share roles on the Kootenay Helpers Instagram and Facebook too.

All of it is free. There are no paid spots, so nobody can buy their way above you.

Know a group that needs Helpers? Send them this.

## d7 Fri Nov 28 carousel: Questions people ask

The questions I get asked most.

Is it free? Yes, for everyone.
Do I need an account? Nope.
Who runs it? Me, Brett, from Castlegar.
Where do the roles come from? The groups' own pages. Every one links back to them.
What about criminal record checks? Your group tells you if you need one, and starts it. Through the province's program it costs a volunteer $0.
Can't commit every week? Some roles are a single shift, like helping at a festival.

Got a question I missed? Ask it in the comments and I'll answer.
