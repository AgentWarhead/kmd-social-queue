## Tue Dec 8, 10 am · for organisers

Your poster can hang on more than one corkboard.

When your event goes on the Slate, it gets a paper of its own. A band night becomes a gig poster. A market hangs as a tag on a string. A class or a workshop goes up as a flyer, like the Ornament Design Make and Take Night at YXC Makerspace in Cranbrook, shown here.

You don't have to design anything. Open kootenayslate.ca/add on your phone, take a photo of the poster you already made, and the wall prints the rest.
