## w1 Sun Nov 29 carousel: Why I built Kootenay Helpers

My wife and I moved to Castlegar in 2023, after ten years in the big city. We fell for it fast. The mountains, the lake, and people who wave.

I build websites. When I looked at how people find volunteer work here, the roles were scattered across hundreds of websites, one group and one town at a time. People want to help. They just can't find where.

Then Nelson at its Best came to me with an idea: put them all in one place. So I built Kootenay Helpers.

It's free, and every role sends you straight back to the group that needs you. If your group needs Helpers, I'd love to have you on it.

## w2 Mon Nov 30 carousel: What happens after you post

You posted a role on Kootenay Helpers. Here's what happens next.

I read it myself, and I reply by email within 24 hours.
Once it's approved, it goes up with the next morning's update.
You get your own edit link, so you can fix a time or close the role yourself. No password.
Every 90 days or so, one email asks if it's still right. One button answers it.

Posting takes about five minutes, and I take it from there.

Save this for the next time your group needs a hand.

## w3 Tue Dec 1 reel: The Kootenay Quilt

Every group on Kootenay Helpers gets a patch on the Kootenay Quilt.

A lantern for the hospice. A steam engine for the history centre. A Dalmatian for one of the fire halls, because every hall should have its own.

Tap a patch and it takes you straight to that group and the Helpers they need.

Run a group? Post a role free and yours joins the quilt.

See the whole quilt at kootenayhelpers.ca.
